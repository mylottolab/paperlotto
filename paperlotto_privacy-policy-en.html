Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026 (revised)

Weapps Solution ("Company") operates PaperLotto ("Service") and establishes and discloses this Privacy Policy in compliance with South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act and other applicable laws, in order to safely manage users' personal information.

1. Age Restriction

This Service is available only to users who are 18 years of age or older. Although PaperLotto does not sell real lottery tickets or pay out real prizes — it is an informational, paper-trading (simulation) service for statistical purposes only — access is restricted to users 18 and older due to the lottery-themed nature of the content. Accounts identified as belonging to users under 18 may be restricted or deleted without prior notice.

3. Purpose of Collection and Use

  • Member identification and login
  • Providing core services such as lottery number registration and self-grading
  • Processing paid (points) services and managing purchase history
  • Analyzing usage statistics to improve service quality
  • Responding to customer inquiries

4. Retention Period

Personal information is destroyed without delay upon account withdrawal, except where retention is required by law, in which case it is retained for the following periods:

  • Records of contracts or withdrawal of subscription: 5 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records of payment and supply of goods/services: 5 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
  • Records of consumer complaints or dispute resolution: 3 years (Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce)
